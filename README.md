## 🏅 Weekly Competition System

### How It Works

Every week, EarlyHunt runs a competition where projects compete for votes and recognition:

- **Start**: Monday 8:00 AM UTC
- **End**: Following Monday 7:59 AM UTC
- **Duration**: Full 7 days of voting
- **Winners**: Top 3 projects with most votes

### What Winners Receive

**All Top 3 Winners Get:**
- 🏆 Exclusive winner badge (downloadable assets)
- 📍 Permanent Hall of Fame placement
- 📈 Increased visibility and credibility
- 📧 Email notification with badge assets

**Free & Premium Launch Winners Also Get:**
- 🔗 Automatic upgrade to **dofollow backlinks**
- 🚀 Enhanced SEO benefits
- ⭐ Priority recognition in search results

**Note**: Nofollow launch winners receive badges but maintain nofollow links as per their launch type.

## 📋 How to Get Started

### Step 1: Create Your Account
1. Visit [earlyhunt.com](https://earlyhunt.com)
2. Click "Sign In" in the header
3. Sign up with email (magic link), Google, or GitHub

### Step 2: Submit Your Project
1. Click "Submit Project" in the navigation
2. Paste your website URL and use **AI Autofill** to save time
3. Complete the submission form with project details
4. Choose your launch type (Free, Nofollow, or Premium)
5. Select your preferred launch week

### Step 3: Complete Requirements
- **Free Launch**: Add EarlyHunt link to your website (any link works)
- **Nofollow Launch**: No requirements needed
- **Premium Launch**: Complete $10 payment

### Step 4: Launch & Compete
- Your project goes live on Monday 8:00 AM UTC
- Share with your network to get votes
- Track performance in your dashboard
- Compete for top 3 and win rewards!

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: Who can submit projects to EarlyHunt?**  
A: Anyone who has built an AI project or digital product can submit. You'll need a working website URL and accurate project information.

**Q: How do I create an account?**  
A: Click "Sign In" and choose email (magic link), Google, or GitHub OAuth. It's free and takes seconds.

**Q: What information do I need to submit?**  
A: Project name, website URL, description, categories, logo, and launch week. Our AI autofill can extract most of this automatically!

### Launch Types & Pricing

**Q: What's the difference between Free, Nofollow, and Premium launches?**  
A: 
- **Free**: $0, badge required, nofollow by default (dofollow if you win)
- **Nofollow**: $0, no badge required, always nofollow
- **Premium**: $10, no badge required, guaranteed dofollow immediately

**Q: Can I change my launch type after submission?**  
A: Yes, but only before your project is scheduled. Once approved and scheduled, you cannot change the launch type.

**Q: Can I relaunch my project if it doesn't win?**  
A: Yes! If your project completes a launch week without winning, you can do a one-time premium relaunch for $10. All existing votes are preserved.

### Weekly Competition

**Q: How does the weekly competition work?**  
A: Projects launch every Monday at 8:00 AM UTC and compete for votes for 7 days. The top 3 projects with the most votes win badges and dofollow links.

**Q: Can I vote for my own project?**  
A: Yes! We encourage creators to share their projects and get votes from their network. Authentic engagement is what matters most.

**Q: How many projects compete each week?**  
A: Each week has 15 available slots for free and nofollow launches combined. Premium launches are unlimited and don't count toward this limit.

### Backlinks & SEO

**Q: What's the difference between nofollow and dofollow backlinks?**  
A: 
- **Nofollow**: Don't pass SEO value to search engines, but still provide traffic
- **Dofollow**: Pass SEO authority and can improve search rankings

**Q: Where will my backlink appear?**  
A: Your backlink appears on your project detail page, homepage listings, browse pages, and related projects sections. All links are permanent.

**Q: How do I verify my backlink for Free launches?**  
A: Add any link to earlyhunt.com on your website (footer, about page, etc.). Our system automatically verifies it by checking your website's source code.

### Payment & Premium

**Q: How does Premium launch payment work?**  
A: Select "Premium Launch" during submission, complete the $10 payment through our secure payment processor (Polar), and your project is automatically scheduled.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**  
A: All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) through Polar. Payments are secure, encrypted, and PCI-compliant.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**  
A: Premium launches are generally non-refundable, but we consider refunds for technical errors, service non-delivery, or duplicate payments. Contact support for assistance.

### Dashboard & Tracking

**Q: What can I see in my dashboard?**  
A: All your submissions, current status, vote counts, rankings, launch dates, and project performance metrics. You can also edit draft submissions and manage your projects.

**Q: Can I edit my project after submission?**  
A: Yes, you can edit draft submissions and scheduled projects (before they go live). You cannot edit website URLs or launch types after submission.

**Q: How do I track votes and performance?**  
A: Your dashboard shows real-time vote counts, ranking positions, and status updates. You'll also receive email notifications for important milestones.

### Platform Policies

**Q: What types of projects are allowed?**  
A: Legitimate AI projects and digital products with working websites that provide value to users. Projects must comply with our Terms of Service.

**Q: Can I submit multiple projects?**  
A: Yes! You can submit as many projects as you like. Each project must have a unique website URL and go through the review process separately.

**Q: What happens to my project after the competition ends?**  
A: Your project remains on EarlyHunt permanently. It stays in the directory, continues to drive traffic, and backlinks remain active for ongoing SEO benefits.
